# Scene brief

Describe the scene in plain words. A paragraph is enough; the beats table is optional and can be filled in by whoever builds it.

## The scene

- **What happens:** one or two sentences. Example: "A seed drops into soil, roots reach down, a sprout opens two leaves."
- **What the viewer should take away:**
- **Where it goes:** page (scroll, click or play button), video file, or both.
- **Format:** 1920 × 1080, 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1920.
- **Length:** in seconds, including at least two seconds of hold on the last frame.
- **Data:** the file or table every number comes from, or "illustrative".
- **Sound:** none, unless the scene is a film over about 15 seconds.

## The last frame

What is on screen when it stops. This frame must make sense as a still image.
