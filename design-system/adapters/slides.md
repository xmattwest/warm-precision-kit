# Slides and presentations

## Purpose and pacing

One argumentative beat per slide. A five-slide explanation can follow: question → design → result → qualification → implication. Use assertion titles instead of category labels. Notes can carry depth, but qualifications required to interpret the result stay visible.

## Geometry and typography

Default authoring canvas: 1600 × 900 (16:9), with 96 px safe margins. Profile body is 32 px, supporting text 24 px, headings 44 px, display 68 px, lede 38 px. Prestige on slides comes from restraint: few short statements, one dominant visual, and titles that fit without shrinking. This replaces document-sized text on projected slides. Judge the final viewing distance; display values are starting points.

Use few short statements and one dominant visual. Charts need slide-specific label sizes and geometry ([data.md](data.md)). Rebuild a figure for a slide rather than enlarging a small screenshot. Use flat Ivory background, dark type, and one accent event. Native slide-export units may differ; preserve proportions and final legibility.

## Delivery and review

A static HTML deck that scrolls vertically, with keyboard navigation and one landscape print page per slide, is the default. Its PDF is the portable export; the HTML is the editable source. Export the PDF from print settings that preserve the 1600 × 900 page size, then open the PDF and check it before sending.

Check every frame at presentation size, sources and sample sizes on results slides, slide order, no clipped content, and one page per slide. For a narrow-screen companion view, stack content with readable text; that companion is not the projected canvas.
